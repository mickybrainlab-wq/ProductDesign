| Phase | Principle | Key Action | Quote |
|-------|-----------|------------|--------|
| 1. Vision | Radical Push | Define a bold, world-changing goal. Sell the future, not the present. | "If you had asked people what they wanted, they would have said a faster horse." |
| 2. Build | Prototype Now | Start building a crude prototype immediately to discover manufacturing challenges. | "The designing of [the rocket] is not hard. The making of it... is hard." |
| 3. Scale | Machine > Product | Focus 90% of your effort on building the production system, not the product itself. | "Building the Starlink production line was... 1000% harder than designing the satellite." |
| 4. Culture | Radical Incentives | Reward success, tolerate failure, and punish a lack of effort. | "If somebody is... failing to innovate... they should either not be promoted or exited." |
| 5. Growth | Speed of Innovation | Measure your success in innovation per year. Focus on rapid, complete iteration. | "Innovation-per-unit time... is what matters." |
